Teaching

Teaching and training

I have taught and mentored students from many walks of life: undergraduates at four-year universities and community colleges, K–12 teachers in workshops, and underrepresented students (e.g., first-generation, low-income, and students with disabilities). Most of my teaching has been through courses at NAU, workshops for students and teachers, and tutoring and mentoring first-generation students, as I am one myself.

I strive to help others understand the complex systems that govern our lives. Rather than checking whether students can repeat back what I teach them, I aim to develop their critical thinking, so that they can understand the best explanations science has to offer.
